Privacy Policy

Play Quiz Games — Akruba Studios — Last updated: June 2026

1. Overview

Akruba Studios ("we", "us", or "our") operates the mobile application Play Quiz Games (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Account information (via Google Sign-In / Firebase Auth):

  • Display name from your Google account
  • Email address
  • Profile photo URL

Game data (stored in Google Firestore):

  • Username and avatar chosen within the App
  • Game progress: conquered countries, stars per category, experience points (XP), gems
  • Language preference (Spanish / English)
  • In-app audio settings (music and sound volume)

Advertising data (via Google AdMob):

  • Advertising identifier (Android Ad ID) used to display relevant ads
  • App usage data for ad personalization, managed entirely by Google LLC

Analytics and session data (via Microsoft Clarity):

  • Anonymized session recordings and interaction heatmaps to improve the App experience
  • No personally identifiable information is extracted from sessions

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, access, or store:

  • Device location or GPS data
  • Camera or microphone data
  • Contacts or phone number
  • Financial or payment information
  • Any data from children under 13 knowingly

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your player profile
  • To save and sync your game progress across sessions
  • To display personalized or non-personalized ads through Google AdMob
  • To analyze in-app behavior and improve usability through Microsoft Clarity
  • To display global rankings (only your chosen username is visible to others)

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Cloud Functions, Analytics) — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google AdMob — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Microsoft Clarity — privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement

6. Data Retention

Your account and game data are retained for as long as you use the App. If you request account deletion, all associated data is permanently removed from our systems within 30 days.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Opt out of personalized advertising through your device's ad settings

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at akruba.studios@gmail.com.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

For questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, contact:
